Release Tracker

For anything that does not come from a search: a tech launch, a merch drop, a tour, a sale. Leave the date empty if it has not been announced. You can edit it later.
Who is tracking it?
People
Everyone here can be tagged on anything you track. Home Assistant sees who tracks what in the by_person attribute of the release sensor. Changes here save right away.
Movies, TV & Celebrities
One TMDB key covers shows, movies, and celebrity searches.
Two-letter country code, like US, GB, or CA.
Games (IGDB)
Free. Log in at dev.twitch.tv/console, register an app, and paste its Client ID and a new Client Secret here. Only needed for games and DLC.
Music & custom items
Nothing to set up. Music comes from MusicBrainz, which is free and needs no key. Follow an artist to see their next announced album, EP, or single, or track one album. Custom items (tech, merch drops, events, sales) use the dates you type in.
MusicBrainz only lists releases once someone has added them, so a brand new announcement can take a while to show up. For those, add it under Anything else.
